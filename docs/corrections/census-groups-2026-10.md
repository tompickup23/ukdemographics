Shares are percentages of all usual residents, as the file stores them (two decimal places); the direct answer and charts round them to one.

Status: draft. Not to be merged before the 1 November 2026 Search Console export (listing test, PR 68). Figures change on published pages, so merging needs Tom's yes and a dated correction note.

## What was wrong

`scripts/transform/transform-ethnicity.mjs` tested for "african" and "caribbean" before it tested for Mixed, so the Census categories "Mixed or Multiple ethnic groups: White and Black African" and "White and Black Caribbean" (2011: "Mixed/multiple ethnic groups: ...") were counted as Black. Its total test also missed the 2011 label "All usual residents", so every 2011 baseline total was 0. Barnsley and Sheffield carried 2011 baselines with no Other group, from an earlier source.

The fix classifies by the high-level group before the colon (`scripts/lib/census-ethnic-groups.mjs`) and rebases only the Census fields from the TS021 and KS201EW extracts (`scripts/build/rebase-census-groups.mjs`; input hashes in `data/raw/manifests/census_ethnic_groups.json`). Modelled areas keep their 2021 shares, which the model wrote from its own base and which were already right.

## Findings and figures this makes stale (not edited here)
